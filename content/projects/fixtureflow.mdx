## Overview

FixtureFlow explores how technology can simplify the operational challenges associated with organising football competitions.

The project brings competition information into a structured platform, reducing reliance on disconnected spreadsheets, manual updates and fragmented communication.

The focus is on creating a practical experience for organisers, clubs, teams and supporters while maintaining a flexible foundation for different competition formats.

## Key engineering areas

- Competition and tournament domain modelling.
- Fixture and results management.
- Standings and competition progression.
- Public-facing information delivery.
- Product architecture.
- User experience design.
