## Overview

The project originated from a personal desire to make better use of time spent driving. Rather than relying entirely on commercially available audiobooks or subscription-based text-to-speech services, I wanted to build a system capable of converting my own learning material into audio.

What started as a simple text-to-speech experiment evolved into a distributed processing architecture, combining a resource-constrained cloud server with a local Mac worker, automatic failover and resumable audio generation.

## Key engineering areas

- Distributed workload processing.
- Resource-constrained infrastructure.
- AI model integration.
- Automatic worker failover.
- Job scheduling and recovery.
- Self-hosted services.
- Performance optimisation.
